01 · about

Adaptive engineer, deterministic by design.

I'm a Computer Science graduate (B.Sc., The Open University of Israel) working as a Full-Stack & Mobile Developer at osApps. There I moved the core analytics pipeline from batch ETL to real-time event streaming on GCP, automated CI/CD across AWS and GCP, and built the APIs, mobile tools and AI systems around it.

I use AI heavily, but carefully. LLMs do the fuzzy work (extraction, translation, sentiment), and every number and decision stays in code I control. Before anything ships, findings are cross-checked by several models: Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT and Grok.

Outside work I do security research: prototype-pollution exploits, an IaC risk scanner in Go & Rego, and CTFs on picoCTF and TryHackMe.

  • Stream, don't batchEvents land in BigQuery in seconds, not the next morning.
  • Models extract, code decidesNo statistic in production is computed by an LLM.
  • Trust, then verifySeveral models checked against each other. When they disagree, a human verifies.
  • Think like an attackerLeast privilege and encryption by default, audited in code.
